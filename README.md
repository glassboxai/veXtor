#Overview

## veXtor — Semantic Guidance and Response Alignment for Large Language Models

**veXtor** is an open-source Artificial Intelligence research project focused on **semantic guidance, response alignment, and semantic drift reduction in Large Language Models (LLMs)**. Developed as part of the experimental **NeXis architecture**, veXtor explores mechanisms for guiding LLM response generation toward the semantic meaning and intent of the user's original query.

Rather than allowing language models to freely expand into loosely related topics, veXtor aims to maintain semantic coherence and relevance throughout the generation process, helping LLMs produce responses that remain focused on the user's question. Its research investigates how semantic guidance and iterative alignment mechanisms can reduce unnecessary digressions, improve response relevance, and support more controlled and inspectable AI behavior.

veXtor is part of [The Glass Box AI Project](https://glassboxai.dev/), an independent open-source research and development initiative dedicated to Explainable AI (XAI), Trustworthy AI, and transparent alternatives to conventional Black Box AI architectures.
